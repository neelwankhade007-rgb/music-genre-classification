Progressive Pooling

Kernels contract from 3×3 down to 2×2 with stride 2 max pooling, achieving translation invariance in both pitch shifts and slight rhythmic variations.

Triple Batch Normalization

Every convolutional block utilizes BatchNormalization, suppressing internal covariate shift and ensuring rapid convergence with stable gradients on spectrogram data.

Dropout Regularization

A 30% dropout rate prior to the final softmax layer prevents the 460k-parameter dense projection from over-memorizing song-specific production artifacts.

Data Leakage Prevention: Track-Level Partitioning

In music information retrieval (MIR), naive segment-level train/test splitting is a well-documented flaw: when 3-second segments of the same song are split randomly between training and testing, models frequently memorize track-specific production timbres or mastering equalizations rather than generalizing genre motifs.

Naive Segment Split (Flawed) Segments from song X appear in both train and test partitions. Test accuracy appears artificially inflated due to composition memorization.
Track-Level Split (Implemented) Tracks are partitioned at the file level (80 tracks train, 20 tracks test per genre). The model has never heard any part of the test tracks, measuring true acoustic generalization.
